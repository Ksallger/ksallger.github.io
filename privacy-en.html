Dicendoria Privacy Policy

Version dated 14 October 2026. Applies to the game version with optional rewarded advertising and no real-money purchases that links to this policy.

1. Who operates the game

Dicendoria is developed by Ksallger, Kazakhstan. For privacy questions, contact dicendoria.support@gmail.com.

This policy describes information handling in the version of the game for which it is published.

2. Information on your device

The game stores progress on your device, including characters, decks, currencies, energy, levels, results, events and claimed rewards. It also stores your nickname, selected avatar and dice skin, a randomly generated local ID, profile creation time according to the device clock, language, audio, vibration and notification preferences. This information supports game progress and your settings.

The device language is used to choose the initial game language. System time is used for game timers. The local ID is not an advertising or hardware identifier. Selecting “Copy ID” places it in the system clipboard; you decide where to paste it.

This version does not automatically send your game progress or profile to the developer. Avatars are selected from built-in images; the game does not request your photos, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

3. Notifications and the in-game inbox

With Android permission, the game can show local reminders about gifts and completed expeditions. Reminder schedules and delivery records are stored on your device. Server push notifications are not used. You can disable reminders in the game or Android settings; notification permission is not required for core gameplay.

The in-game inbox displays content included in the game and locally records message receipt, read status and claimed rewards. Device time and profile creation time determine message availability; a display deadline does not automatically delete those records. It does not access your email inbox.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Rewarded ads are requested by you for an extra shop gift, energy or ruins shovels. Viewing ads is not required for core gameplay. Debug builds may use a clearly labelled simulated ad without an advertising request.

Google may receive IP addresses (including to estimate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, Android advertising IDs, App Set IDs and other applicable device/account identifiers. These are used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK transmissions use TLS. The game does not request precise location. Our advertising adapter does not send your saved game profile to Google.

UMP checks whether notices and consent are required. Ad requests are permitted only when the SDK's canRequestAds() allows them. Where required, an Advertising privacy button in settings lets you change your choices. Declining personalisation does not necessarily disable all advertising: availability of non-personalised or limited ads depends on your choices and Google's applicable rules. Android settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID.

An age group is stored locally to configure advertising; no date of birth is requested. Ads are disabled below age 13. Ages 13–15 are tagged as under the age of consent. The advertising SDK receives the applicable flag, not a date of birth. Maximum ad content rating is configured as PG.

Google processes information under the Google Privacy Policy, potentially outside your country. Google's policies determine its retention and data controls; the developer cannot promise deletion of data outside their control. See Google's use of information from partner apps. Consent may be the legal basis where required; applicable rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction and complaints to a supervisory authority remain unaffected.

Real-money purchases, Google Play Games and cloud saves are not integrated. The local profile is not an online account.

5. Support requests

If you choose to email dicendoria.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your sender address, message and attachments you provide. Do not send passwords, full payment credentials or unnecessary personal information. Support uses Gmail, whose processing is subject to its terms.

Support requests are used to answer questions, investigate and resolve issues, and handle personal information requests. On the developer's side, only Ksallger has access. Messages and attachments are deleted from the support mailbox, including its trash, no later than 90 days after the request is resolved, unless law requires certain information to be retained longer. Unresolved requests are retained while work on them is necessary. Technical deletion of copies held by Gmail is governed by Google's policies.

We aim to reply within 3–5 days. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed deadline. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information supplied to support, email the same address, preferably from the address used for the original request. We may ask for minimal information needed to confirm your connection to the request; passwords are not needed. Mandatory legal rights and deadlines remain unaffected.

The support button opens your chosen email app with the recipient address and a subject containing the game name and version. It does not automatically send a message or attach your game profile or logs. Gmail is provided by Google; email may be processed outside your country under Google's privacy policy.

6. Storage, security and deletion

Saves are stored in the application’s data area, access to which is normally restricted by Android. The game uses local files and does not claim separate application-level encryption of saves. No storage method provides absolute security. Format migrations may retain full archival copies of progress and profile data for recovery, without automatic time-based deletion. Changing a nickname does not necessarily update its value in older copies.

You can delete local progress and settings by using Clear app data/storage in Android. Clearing only the cache is not equivalent to deleting progress. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but does not guarantee deletion of all previously created backups or copies transferred by device tools.

Android cloud Auto Backup is disabled in this version. Manufacturer transfer tools and older backups may behave differently. The developer does not gain access to them through the game. Support cannot remotely remove local files from your phone.

Send requests concerning personal information you supplied to support to the email address above. This is separate from deleting a local game profile.

7. Audience and changes

Dicendoria is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults. Users select an age group for advertising; no documentary age verification is performed. This intended audience does not mean every user has reached adulthood or the age of independent consent to data processing in their country.

A parent or legal guardian may contact dicendoria.support@gmail.com about information a minor has provided to support. Do not send information about a child that is unnecessary to resolve the request.

The policy will be updated when data practices change. The current version will be available at the published privacy policy URL.
